2. Story text (timing ke saath)

Sabse sahi tareeka: apni story neeche paste karo, phir "Audio se text nikalo" dabao. Whisper sirf timing legi, subtitle mein aapka sahi text aayega. Story paste nahi ki to Whisper ka apna text aayega, jo Hindi mein galat ho sakta hai (Tiny model sabse kamzor hai).

Captions

Hinglish apne aap ban jaata hai (Devanagari text se). English ke liye neeche apna English text paste karo (sabse sahi), ya khali chhodo to Whisper audio se translate karega (kam sahi).

3. Story samjho (auto)

ON: narration (jaise "subah ka samay tha") par koi character nahi bolta, "🎙 Narrator" dikhta hai. Dialogue (jaise "khargosh ne kaha, ...") par sirf wahi character bolta hai, caption peela dikhta hai. OFF: pehle jaisa, har awaaz par character bolta hai.
Story ke shabdon se (subah, raat, jungle, mahal, gaon...) scene aur background chunta hai, aur kirdaar dhoondhta hai. Neeche se jo characters chahiye wo chuno.

Action, emotion aur saaman

Auto: story ke shabdon se (chalta, daudta, baitha, so gaya, kha raha, ro raha, khush, gussa, dar, hairan, kulhadi, chaaku, talwaar, banduk, lathi). Emotion 3 line baad apne aap normal ho jaata hai. Neeche har line ke liye khud bhi badal sakte ho ("Auto" ka matlab story se pehchana hua).
